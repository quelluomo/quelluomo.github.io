About

I started in front-end development, moved into product design, and found my footing where design meets language: making products clear, consistent, and inclusive for the people using them.

These days I write the technical documentation for an enterprise CX platform — feature docs, component references, tutorials, and operational guides. I also design and deliver internal training for audiences of up to 50 people at once, from call-centre operators to operations and sales teams.

Along the way I've built a UX writing system from nothing, owned the English localisation of a bilingual product, and learned to design for the messy reality of enterprise constraints. I care about the small words on the screen, because they add up.
