SUPABOY BURGERS

Designing a mobile ordering app for a Sardinian gourmet burger food truck to improve the customer experience and overcome language barriers.

Project

SupaBoy Burgers Mobile App

What I Did

  • User Research
  • UX Design
  • UI Design
  • Wireframing
  • Prototyping

Tools

Figma, Paper Prototyping, User Testing

Timeline

2023

The Challenge

SupaBoy Burgers, a food truck based in Cagliari, Sardinia, specializes in gourmet burgers. Customers found the ordering process limited and cumbersome, especially international customers who faced language barriers. The business lacked a dedicated application and needed to expand their user base while improving the overall ordering experience.

The Solution

I designed a mobile ordering app that serves diverse customer segments with a focus on accessibility and multilingual support. The app streamlines the ordering process with real-time tracking, reducing the need for in-person counter interactions and eliminating language-related miscommunication.

The Work

User Research

I conducted user research with current and potential customers to understand their needs and diversify the app to cater to a more diverse user base. Through persona development and empathy mapping, I identified key pain points in the current ordering experience.

A critical insight emerged from the Elodie persona—a foreigner who needed translation assistance during ordering. This revealed that language barriers were causing miscommunication and order errors, particularly affecting international customers visiting Sardinia.

Defining the Problem

Based on the research findings, I identified four main pain points that needed to be addressed:

  • Wait Time Frustration: Customers who are at the restaurant to pick an order find frustrating to have to wait in line alongside customers who still need to order
  • Lack of Dedicated App: Users find the lack of a dedicated app inconvenient
  • Limited In-Person Interactions: Some customers would like to limit their in-person interactions (language barriers or other reasons)
  • Order Tracking: Customers would like to track their orders for curbside pickup

The goal was to reduce in-person counter interactions while maintaining the quality of service and expanding the customer base to include tourists and international residents.

Design Process

Paper Wireframes

I started with paper wireframes to establish a coherent user flow for ordering and real-time tracking. This low-fidelity approach allowed for rapid iteration and testing of different layout concepts before committing to digital designs.

Digital Wireframes

The digital wireframes prioritized relevant information at a glance, allowing users to quickly order and track their food. Supplementary details were placed on the homepage using lower visual hierarchy to avoid overwhelming users with information.

Usability Testing

I conducted two rounds of usability studies where participants placed orders and tracked completion. The testing revealed opportunities to streamline the tracking interface and improve information clarity.

Key insights from testing led to improvements in the order tracker, making it more intuitive and providing users with clearer status updates throughout the preparation process.

Design Principles

Multilingual Support

Built-in translation capabilities to serve international customers and eliminate language barriers during ordering.

Clear Information Hierarchy

Prioritized essential ordering information while making supplementary details easily accessible without overwhelming users.

Accessibility First

Incorporated accessibility considerations throughout the design to ensure the app works for all users.

Key Features

Streamlined Order Tracker

The order tracker was designed with enhanced information clarity, showing real-time status updates from order placement through preparation to pickup. Visual indicators help users understand exactly where their order is in the process.

Intuitive Menu Navigation

Clear categorization and visual representations of menu items make browsing and selection effortless, even for first-time users or those unfamiliar with Italian cuisine.

Customization Options

Users can easily customize their burgers with add-ons and modifications, with clear visual feedback on their choices.

Impact & Learnings

The SupaBoy Burgers app successfully addressed the core challenges identified during research:

✓
Improved Ordering Experience

Streamlined the ordering process with intuitive navigation and real-time tracking

✓
Language Barriers Eliminated

Multilingual support enabled international customers to order confidently

✓
Reduced Counter Interactions

Self-service ordering decreased wait times and staff workload

✓
Expanded User Base

Designed for diverse customer segments including tourists and locals

Key Takeaway: This project reinforced the importance of inclusive design and understanding diverse user needs. By focusing on accessibility and multilingual support from the start, we created a solution that not only solved immediate problems but also opened up new business opportunities for SupaBoy Burgers.
